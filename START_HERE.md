## First-time setup

- Quick start: open the included `Pathcraft.html` in a browser.
- Recommended: extract `Pathcraft_Source.zip`, enter its `Pathcraft` folder and run:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory dist
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000`, bookmark it and keep using that exact address. Keep the terminal running while studying. Do not switch between the downloaded file and localhost expecting their progress to match.

Export a JSON backup after important work and during your weekly review. Hosting alone does not synchronise devices. No cloud service, automatic reminder or phone blocker has been configured for you.

**Weekly:** review practical proof, eligible applications, screens/interviews and repeated blockers. Choose one repair. Keep new-roadmap ideas parked until that review.

**Missed a day?** Resume with the saved action. No catch-up debt. No progress reset.
